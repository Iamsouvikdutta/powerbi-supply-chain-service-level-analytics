# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Service Level Insights

- OTIF performance is significantly below the business target, indicating opportunities for operational improvement.
- On-Time Delivery performance is the primary contributor to OTIF shortfalls.
- In-Full performance remains relatively stable compared to delivery timeliness.

## Operational Insights

- Certain cities consistently underperform against service-level benchmarks.
- Product fulfillment performance varies across categories, highlighting potential process inefficiencies.
- Customer service levels differ significantly, requiring targeted interventions.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Delivery Execution

Focus on route optimization and dispatch planning to enhance On-Time performance.

### 2. Prioritize Low-Performing Cities

Develop city-specific action plans for regions consistently missing OTIF targets.

### 3. Strengthen Customer-Level Monitoring

Track service-level KPIs at the customer level to proactively identify risks.

### 4. Enhance Performance Reviews

Implement regular reviews of OTIF, OT, and IF metrics to support continuous improvement.

### 5. Investigate Root Causes

Conduct detailed analyses to understand the factors contributing to delayed deliveries and fulfillment gaps.

## Expected Business Impact

- Improved customer satisfaction through better service levels.
- Enhanced operational efficiency.
- Data-driven decision-making for supply chain improvements.
- Better visibility into fulfillment performance across the organization.
